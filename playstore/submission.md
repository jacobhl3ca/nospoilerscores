## Store listing

Short description:

> Watch sports spoiler-free. Scores hidden by default, plus matchup ratings.

Full description:

> HideScore lets you catch up on sports without seeing the score.
>
> Pick a date, hide the result, and watch a game like it's live—even when it's already over. Competitiveness ratings tell you which games are worth your time before you commit to watching.
>
> KEY FEATURES
> • Scores hidden by default—you control when to reveal them
> • Competitiveness ratings to find games worth watching
> • Today, tomorrow, and past dates
> • Highlights, news, and live game links when you're ready
> • Favorite-team filters
> • No accounts, tracking, or ads
>
> HideScore is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any team, league, broadcaster, or sports organization.
